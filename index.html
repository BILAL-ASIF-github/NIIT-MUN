About NIIT

A premier center of technology, leadership, and intellectual excellence

The NASTP Institute of Information Technology (NIIT), a constituent college of Air University, offers programs in Computer Science, AI, Software Engineering, and Cyber Security. Located in the National Aerospace Science and Technology Park (NASTP Delta Lahore), NIIT combines a cutting-edge curriculum, expert faculty, and modern facilities to prepare graduates for leadership in the digital economy.

With the proud patronage of the Pakistan Air Force and an unwavering commitment to scholastic rigor, NIIT provides delegates with an inspiring academic environment designed to foster critical inquiry, constructive debate, and actionable solutions for global peace and development.

Socials & Activities

Three signature evenings of music, traditional culture, and grand diplomatic celebration

Day 1 • 4 December 2026

Jamming Session

Acoustic Live Music • Delegate Icebreaker

Kick off NIITMUN with an electrifying acoustic jamming session. Unwind after an intense opening day of debate, showcase your musical talents, and forge lifelong bonds with delegates from across Pakistan in a relaxed, vibrant atmosphere.

Day 2 • 5 December 2026

Social Qawwali Night

Sufi Harmony • Cultural Extravaganza

Immerse yourself in the transcendent melodies of authentic classical Sufi music. Our signature social evening brings delegates together under the stars for an enchanting night of soulful rhythm, poetic devotion, and traditional hospitality.

Day 3 • 6 December 2026

Formal Delegate Dinner

Closing Gala • Award Distribution

Celebrate the conclusion of three days of high-stakes diplomacy with an exquisite formal banquet. Reflect on diplomatic triumphs, honor outstanding committee performances during the award ceremony, and celebrate the grand closing of NIITMUN.
